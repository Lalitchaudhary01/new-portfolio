about.md

Engineer first, product-minded always.

I'm a full-stack developer who likes owning a product end to end, from the database schema to the last pixel of the interface.

Most of my time goes into OpenTreatment, a price-transparency marketplace that helps self-pay patients in Delhi NCR find verified doctors, hospitals and labs and see what they will pay upfront. The platform is built and run in-house, across Android, iOS and the web.

Outside of that I build tools I want to exist: a real-time collaborative code editor, a product discovery engine, and a steady stream of experiments with Next.js and generative AI. I care about performance, clean architecture and interfaces that feel obvious.

I also document the build in public, one day at a time, on @lalitbuildsdaily.

OpenTreatment Patient App

The patient app for OpenTreatment, built in React Native. Patients compare and book doctors, hospitals and lab tests, track every visit, and keep their family's health records in one place.

  • Compare and book doctors, hospital OPD and lab tests, with upfront pricing and payment
  • Live 5-stage appointment tracking, from confirmation to consultation
  • Digital prescriptions and health records, with a family profile switcher
  • Secure token-based auth with silent refresh, plus Redis caching for fast listings
